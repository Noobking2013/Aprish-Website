## 1. Q&A sheet (ten hard questions)

**Q1. How is Aprish different from Practo or eka.care?**  
Most clinic software digitizes single tasks. Aprish is built to improve clinic economics: one platform that starts with one module (WhatsApp booking, live today) and expands, with pay-per-module pricing and one published price list. **Q2. Why will clinics pay?**  
Because the value is measurable: monthly value = no-shows avoided per day × working days × average consultation fee. Break-even = plan price ÷ average fee, i.e. the visits needed to recover the plan each month. Our numbers: [FILL: average fee, no-shows per day, working days].

**Q3. How do you reach 3,000+ clinics?**  
Anchor doctors first, then their peers (slide 9). Channels: field demos and pilots, the live Aria demo, partner routing, content/SEO, associations and events, LinkedIn. The 3,000–7,000 range is derived as [FILL: derivation].

**Q4. What happens if the AI makes an error?**  
Aria understands; the booking engine decides. Gemini only interprets intent. The booking engine (slot-locking, rules, audit log) makes every booking decision, and the AI never books, cancels or changes roles by itself, so an AI misreading cannot change a calendar on its own.
