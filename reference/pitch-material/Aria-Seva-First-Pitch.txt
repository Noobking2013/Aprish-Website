Aria
A WhatsApp-First AI Clinic Operations Platform for Haryana


 Sector: Healthcare                           Submitted for: Seva First Innovation Challenge 2026                                    Haryana



The Problem
Across Haryana's private and semi-urban clinics, appointment booking still runs on phone calls that go unanswered,
WhatsApp DMs staff read late, or walk-in queues with no visibility into wait time. Patients have no way to know if a
doctor is running late, so they either arrive on time and wait for hours, or miss their slot and struggle to reschedule.
Clinic staff juggle registers, spreadsheets, and disconnected apps to track schedules, patient records, and prescriptions,
with no single shared source of truth. Smaller clinics cannot afford custom software built one clinic at a time, so most
simply go without any digital system, directly affecting quality of care and public trust in local healthcare delivery.

The Solution
Aria is a WhatsApp-first, AI-assisted clinic operations platform that needs zero app download: booking, rescheduling,
check-in, and doctor-delay alerts happen inside a chat patients already have open. A deterministic booking engine, not
free-form AI, handles every critical action, slot locking, confirmation, queue position, and prescriptions, so patients
always get an accurate, auditable outcome, while Gemini AI is used only to understand natural language and assist
staff, never to make bookings itself. One unified backend serves the WhatsApp bot, an admin/doctor/staff web
dashboard, and future mobile apps from the same live data, and the system is multi-tenant from day one so a single
deployment can onboard many clinics across Haryana without rebuilding software per clinic.

Why It's Different
Zero-install, WhatsApp-native access reaches patients who won't install a dedicated app. AI never holds authority
— Gemini interprets language only; every booking, cancellation, delay, and role change runs through deterministic,
auditable logic, removing the hallucination risk of typical AI-chatbot health tools. One shared backend across every
channel means a clinic never has two versions of the truth. Multi-tenant by design lets Haryana onboard hundreds of
clinics under one deployment, not one-off custom builds.

Expected Impact
Shorter patient wait times and fewer missed appointments through real-time queue and delay visibility. Less
administrative burden on clinic staff, freeing time for patient care. Basic digital record-keeping for clinics that previously
had none, laying groundwork for future integration with ABHA / Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. Because it runs on
WhatsApp, the reach extends to older patients and smaller towns that an app-only or website-only solution would miss
— and because it's multi-tenant, the same platform scales from a handful of pilot clinics to district- or state-wide
coverage at minimal marginal engineering cost.


Current build: Python/FastAPI backend, SQLite, Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API, Google Gemini, optional Google Calendar sync —
already implements multi-tenant clinics, doctor scheduling and breaks, deterministic appointment booking with slot-locking, OPD queue and
check-in, delay broadcast, digital prescriptions, and role-based access (Owner / Admin / Staff / Doctor / Patient). Web dashboard and public
API layer in active development on the same unified backend.
